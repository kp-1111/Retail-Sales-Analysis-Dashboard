# 📊 Canadian Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes **Canadian retail trade data** using Statistics Canada datasets.  
The goal is to identify **consumer spending trends, seasonal patterns, and growth rates** across provinces.

The dashboard is built in **Power BI** and designed for **business insights and decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **national retail sales trends**
- Compare **provincial performance**
- Identify **seasonal patterns in consumer spending**
- Calculate **Year-over-Year (YoY) growth rates**
- Build an **interactive and executive-level dashboard**

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: **Statistics Canada – Retail Trade Data**
- Frequency: **Monthly**
- Coverage:
  - Multi-year retail sales data
  - Provincial-level breakdown
  - Industry categories (if applicable)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development & visualization  
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning & preprocessing  
- **Statistics Canada Data** – Primary data source  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. National Trends
- Overall retail sales over time  
- Long-term growth patterns  
- Trend line visualization  

### 2. Provincial Comparison
- Retail sales by province  
- Performance comparison across regions  
- Identification of high/low-performing provinces  

### 3. Seasonal Patterns
- Monthly sales trends  
- Detection of peak seasons (e.g., holidays)  
- Recurring consumer behavior patterns  

### 4. Growth Analysis
- Year-over-Year (YoY) growth calculation  
- Trend comparison across years  
- Growth rate insights for decision-making  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Retail sales show **seasonal spikes during holiday periods**
- Some provinces consistently outperform others
- Clear **cyclical patterns** in consumer spending
- YoY growth highlights **recovery and expansion trends**
